Purchase and licence help

Licence enquiries and activation

This page does not verify payment. Online checkout is not available yet. For a new licence, contact sales to confirm available payment options before sending payment. After we verify a licence purchase, we email your key within one business day. Keys are prepared manually, not generated by visiting this page.

Already arranged a payment?

If you have already paid, keep your receipt and use its email address and payment reference when contacting sales@workforcetimetracker.com. Do not send card details.

An optional contribution supports development; it does not activate or renew a licence. For continued use after the trial, choose a licence plan.

What happens next

  1. After payment is verified, you receive an email from sales@workforcetimetracker.com with your signed licence key. Check your spam folder if it has not arrived.
  2. If you haven't already, download Workforce Time Tracker from the download page.
  3. On Windows, open Settings → LICENSE, paste the key and save. On macOS, open About / License, paste the key and activate it.
  4. The same key works on Windows AND macOS. One licence covers both platforms; copy it to any machine where you're the user.

While you wait

If your free 14-day trial is still active, you can keep tracking while your key is prepared. No card is required for the trial. Your time entries stay on your device; buying a licence does not move them to a cloud account.
