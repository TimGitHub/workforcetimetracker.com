Why people search for a Clockify alternative

Reason 1

Cloud account fatigue

Your billable-hours log doesn't need to be in someone else's database. You don't sign up to Clockify, you don't accept their privacy policy, you don't get the breach email three years from now.

Reason 2

Per-seat subscription forever

Five users on Clockify Standard is roughly EUR 329/year - every year, forever. Pay this app once per user, use it for years.

Reason 3

SaaS approval is months away

Regulated industries (healthcare, legal, defence, government, finance) need vendor risk assessment, DPA review, and procurement sign-off for every new SaaS. A desktop app installed on your own machine sidesteps that entirely.

Reason 4

You want true multi-device sync without giving up control

The app stores its data in JSON files in a folder you control. Point it at the Dropbox / OneDrive / iCloud / SyncThing / NAS folder you already sync, and your time entries follow you across all your devices - using sync infrastructure you already trust.

Reason 5

You want CRM-aware auto-tracking

Clockify's auto-tracker logs raw window titles. The Salesforce / Dynamics 365 editions of this app parse the record name out of the tab title and pre-fill the timer. Window-title parsing only - no API integration.

Reason 6

You're already on Mac + Windows

Same product on both platforms, one licence covers both. Multi-device sync handles the cross-platform handoff automatically through your existing sync folder.

Common questions about migrating from Clockify

Can I import my Clockify data?

Yes. The app reads the Clockify CSV export directly. Export your detailed report from Clockify, drop the CSV into the app, and your historical entries are imported into local storage. Customer names and tags are preserved.

Is Workforce Time Tracker a true alternative to Clockify?

For individual freelancers and small teams who want billable-hours tracking, customer/project hours, idle detection, weekly approvals and PDF / Excel / CSV export - yes, the feature parity is high. For 50+ person teams that need cloud-side admin, SSO, SCIM, scheduling, or a mobile app for clock-in - no, Clockify wins those use cases.

Why would I switch from Clockify to a desktop app?

Three common reasons: (1) you don't want your billable hours stored in someone else's cloud; (2) you'd rather pay once per user than a per-seat monthly subscription forever; (3) you work in a regulated industry (healthcare, legal, defence, government) where any new SaaS vendor requires months of approval. The desktop app removes the SaaS approval problem entirely.

How does multi-device sync work without a cloud account?

Point the app at a folder you already sync with Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud Drive, SyncThing, or a NAS share. The app puts its JSON data files in that folder. Your existing sync tool replicates them to your other devices, where the app reads from the same folder. No new vendor account, no new attack surface.

What is the catch?

Two trade-offs vs Clockify: (1) no mobile app - this is a Windows / macOS desktop product only, so on-the-go clock-in must wait until you're at your laptop; (2) no team admin console - team management is currently file-based (shared customers folder), not a hosted UI. If either of those is a hard requirement, Clockify is the right tool for you. Otherwise, the desktop app probably wins.

How much does it cost vs Clockify?

EUR 5 per user per month, or EUR 48 per user per year (save 20%). For comparison, Clockify Standard is EUR 5.49/seat/month and Pro is EUR 7.99/seat/month. Slightly cheaper at the monthly tier, much cheaper over multiple years (no perpetual subscription growth).
