Mac first-launch instructions

Workforce Time Tracker on macOS needs two one-time clicks before it runs. After this, it behaves like any other Mac app.

Why these steps exist (one honest paragraph) We ship Workforce Time Tracker as an indie app. To make every Mac install completely warning-free, Apple charges us €99/year for the Developer Program. We will pay for that as soon as Mac sales justify the cost. Until then, macOS shows a "could not be verified" warning the first time you launch the app — the workaround below takes 30 seconds.

Step 1 — Download and open

1

Download the .dmg

Click Download for Mac on the home page. Open the .dmg in Finder and drag Workforce Time Tracker into your Applications folder.

2

Try to launch the app

Double-click Workforce Time Tracker in Applications. macOS shows a dialog: "Apple could not verify Workforce Time Tracker is free of malware..."

Click Cancel. (Do not click "Move to Trash".)

Step 2 — Allow it through Gatekeeper

3

Open Privacy & Security settings

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll to the bottom of the page until you see a section about Workforce Time Tracker being blocked.

4

Click "Open Anyway"

Next to the message, click Open Anyway. macOS will ask for confirmation — click Open. The app launches.

That's it From now on the app launches normally with no warnings. macOS has whitelisted it.

Step 3 — Grant Accessibility permission (only if you want auto-detect)

Workforce Time Tracker can auto-detect which Salesforce account, Dynamics 365 case, or Jira ticket you're working on by reading the active window title. macOS requires Accessibility permission for this. If you skip this step, you can still track time manually.

5

Open Accessibility settings

In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, click the + button.

6

Add Workforce Time Tracker

Navigate to Applications → Workforce Time Tracker and add it. Toggle the switch on.

7

Restart the app

Quit Workforce Time Tracker (Cmd+Q) and re-launch it. The auto-detect banner will start firing when you open Salesforce / D365 / Jira / Outlook windows.

FAQ

Will my license work on both my Mac and my Windows machine?

Yes. One license, both platforms. Same license key, same activation flow. Activate it on as many of your own devices as you need.

Where is my data stored?

~/Library/Application Support/WorkforceTimeTracker/ — settings, customers, time entries, and license. Nothing leaves your Mac.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

The current build targets Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) only. If you have an Intel Mac and would like to use Workforce Time Tracker, please email support@workforcetimetracker.com and we will produce an Intel build.

What macOS version do I need?

macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer.

Can I use AppleScript to read browser tabs?

Not yet. The current build reads the title of the active app window. Browser-tab URL detection (Safari, Chrome) is on the roadmap.

What about an Apple notarized build with no warning?

Coming once the app has 5 paying Mac customers — that justifies the €99/year Apple Developer Program fee. Existing users will get the smoother experience automatically as a free update.

Something is broken — who do I contact?

Email support@workforcetimetracker.com. We aim to reply within 24 hours.
