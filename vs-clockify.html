Workforce Time Tracker vs Clockify

Both are great products. They are not the same product. Clockify is the world's most popular team SaaS tracker. We are the offline, one-time-purchase desktop tool for people who refuse to upload their working hours. Here is when each one wins.

Pillar 1

Your data stays on your machine

Plain JSON files next to the .exe. No cloud account, no telemetry, no DPA paperwork. Your IT department doesn't have to approve a SaaS vendor. Clockify is hosted SaaS by design - that is their whole business.

Pillar 2

One-time license, not a subscription

Pay once and use it forever. Clockify Standard at EUR 5.49/seat/month is EUR 329/year for a 5-person team - every year, forever. We charge per user once.

Pillar 3

CRM-aware, not just window-title-aware

Open a Salesforce Case or Dynamics 365 record - we extract the customer name and pre-fill the timer. Optional auto-start fires it for you. Clockify's auto-tracker logs raw window titles you have to clean up afterwards.
