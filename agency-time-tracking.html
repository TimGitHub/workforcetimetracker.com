What an agency actually does with this

Workflow 1

Onboarding a new project

Producer adds the customer + project + budget to the shared customers folder. Within minutes, every team member's tracker sees it. They can start logging.

Workflow 2

Tracking through the week

Each team member tracks locally on their machine. CRM tab auto-detection prefils customer when working in Salesforce / Jira / Asana / Linear / Monday.com. Idle prompt catches gaps.

Workflow 3

Friday submit

Each team member submits the week. Entries lock. Producer's Pending Approvals window shows everyone's week in one list.

Workflow 4

Producer review

Producer scans the submitted weeks, approves or rejects each (with a note). Rejected weeks return to draft for the team member to fix and re-submit.

Workflow 5

Invoicing

Per customer, generate a branded PDF invoice. Line items per project. Customer-specific rate. Hand off to your accountant or bill direct.

Workflow 6

End-of-month roll-up

Excel export gives total hours per customer per project per team member. Drop into your finance/utilisation spreadsheet of choice.

Common agency questions

How big does my agency need to be for this?

Any size, including solo founders and 50+ person agencies. Buy one per-user licence per person on the team. Customers, projects and defaults are shared via the local admin console and a folder you already replicate (Dropbox / OneDrive / iCloud / SyncThing / NAS). Cloud-side admin console, SSO and SCIM aren't included today; everything is managed locally.

How does the team share customers and projects?

Point every team member at a shared customers folder on Dropbox / OneDrive / SMB share. Adding a customer once propagates to everyone. Each person tracks locally and submits weekly to the same shared timesheet folder.

Can a partner or producer approve everyone's weeks?

Yes. Buy a manager licence for the partner/producer. They see a Pending Approvals window listing every submitted week from the team, with approve/reject + note. Rejected weeks return to draft for the team member to fix.

Does it warn when a project budget is about to blow?

Yes. Set a budget per project (in hours or in EUR). The app warns at 80% and 100% of the budget across all team members combined. No more end-of-project surprises.

Why pick this over a hosted PSA?

Your billable hours stay under your own control. One per-user licence covers everyone in the team, time entries live in plain JSON files on your machines, and you keep the sync stack you already use (Dropbox / OneDrive / iCloud / SyncThing / NAS). No per-seat-per-month SaaS spend, no vendor cloud holding your client list, no DPA paperwork.

Can the whole team trial it before we buy?

Yes. The 14-day free trial is per install, not per organisation. Drop the .exe (Windows) or .app (macOS) on every team member's machine, point them all at the shared data folder, and each install runs a full-feature trial for 14 days from its first launch - including the manager approval workflow and project budgets. No credit card, no email required to start. Convert to paid by buying one per-user licence per person and pasting the key into Settings on each machine.
