Common consultant questions

Does it auto-detect customers from my CRM tab?

Yes - the app reads the title of your active browser tab on your own machine and parses out the record name. Salesforce, Dynamics 365, Jira, ServiceNow, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Asana, Linear and Monday.com are all supported. It never logs into the CRM, never calls any API, and never installs anything inside it.

Can I lock submitted weeks so accidental edits do not change historical bills?

Yes. When you submit a week, every entry is marked locked and the edit dialog refuses to save changes. Rejected weeks return to draft so you can fix and re-submit. This is essential when audit trails matter.

Does it support manager approval workflow?

Yes - optional. A manager licence sees a Pending Approvals window listing submitted weeks across the team and can approve or reject each with a note. By default the workflow is off (single-user / freelancer mode auto-approves on submit).

Can I bill different customers at different hourly rates?

Yes. Each customer record carries its own hourly rate. The PDF invoice and Excel export apply the customer-specific rate per line.

Can I work across multiple devices?

Yes. Point the app at any folder you already sync (Dropbox / OneDrive / iCloud / SyncThing / NAS) and the time entries follow you across desktop, laptop, Mac and Windows.

I already use another time tracker. Can I bring my history?

Clockify CSV: yes, one click - Settings > Import Clockify CSV. Use the "Detailed report" CSV export; the importer maps Project to Client (with a Client-column fallback), creates the customer list automatically, and writes the entries into your local time-entries store.
Other SaaS trackers: no dedicated importer yet. Export from your current tool to CSV, rename the columns to match the Clockify Detailed-report format (the importer is forgiving about exact header names), and run the same import. Native importers for additional formats are on the roadmap.
