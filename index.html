v1.7.0 - May 2026

Stop giving away free hours

v1.7 adds the two things consultants and freelancers asked for most after v1.6: a project budget so you stop bleeding hours on fixed-fee work, and a daily goal so you actually hit your billable target. Plus three real security hardenings on the v1.6 sync surface. Free upgrade for every existing customer.

1

Project budgets

Set a fixed-fee budget in hours per client. The main window shows live progress while you work and warns at 80% and 100%. Stop discovering at month-end that you put 60 hours into a 40-hour contract.

2

Daily goal

Tell us your target billable hours per day. A small progress bar in the main window keeps you honest in real time - amber when behind, green when on track, blue when you've done enough.

3

Sync conflict detection

If Dropbox / iCloud / OneDrive / SyncThing creates a "conflicted copy" file (two devices saved at the same moment), v1.7 spots it on launch and tells you. v1.6 silently ignored these.

4

Sync folder unreachable banner

If your Dropbox is offline / NAS unmounted / sync folder typo'd, v1.7 falls back to local data AND shows you why. v1.6 just silently fell back, leaving people staring at an empty workspace.

5

Pinned download domain

The auto-update flow now refuses to download from anywhere except workforcetimetracker.com over HTTPS. Even if our website were compromised, attackers couldn't redirect users to a malicious binary.

6

CSV formula injection guarded

Cells starting with =, +, -, @, tab or CR are prefixed with an apostrophe so a malicious customer name in your data can't execute formulas in Excel/LibreOffice/Numbers.

7

Lock-file display sanitization

The session lock now strips control codes, bidi marks and line separators before showing another device's hostname/username, so a malicious lock dropped on a shared NAS can't spoof or corrupt the conflict toast.

8

Everything from v1.5 / v1.6 still here

Multi-device sync, week calendar, idle prompt, PDF invoicing, Pomodoro, CRM auto-start, Clockify import, hourly rates … nothing removed. v1.7 just makes them safer and more useful.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The app is 100% offline. Your license is validated locally — no server contact ever.

Where is my data stored?

By default, in the same folder as the .exe (Windows) or under ~/Library/Application Support/WorkforceTimeTracker/ (macOS), as plain JSON files you can read with any text editor. In v1.6 you can also point it at a folder inside Dropbox / OneDrive / iCloud / SyncThing / a NAS share to sync across all your own devices — with no cloud account from us.

How does multi-device sync work without a server?

You pick a folder you already sync (Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud Drive, SyncThing, or a network share). We move our JSON files into that folder. Your existing sync tool replicates them. The app on each device reads from there, gets a banner if another device is editing concurrently, and reloads automatically when the other device saves. No new accounts. No new third party. No new attack surface.

Can I use this for a team?

Yes. Buy multiple seats and use the admin console to share customer lists and defaults.

What if my license expires?

14-day grace period, then a renewal reminder. Your data is never locked.

Can I transfer to another machine?

Copy the .exe and data file. Your license works on any number of your own devices.

Is there a Mac version?

Yes - macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon, currently in ALPHA. Same product, same data files, one license unlocks both platforms. The Mac build is feature-complete (week calendar, idle prompt, PDF invoicing, Clockify import - everything the Windows build has), but it is ad-hoc-signed (Gatekeeper needs one click on first launch) and we are still hardening the Mac-specific bits like Accessibility permission flow and Login Item registration. Report bugs at support@workforcetimetracker.com.
