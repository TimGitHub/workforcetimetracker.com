FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The app is 100% offline. Your license key is validated using local cryptography — no server is ever contacted.

Where is my data stored?

In the same folder as the .exe file as a simple JSON file. You can back it up, move it, or delete it at any time.

Can I use this for my team?

Yes! Buy seats for your team and use the admin console to share client lists and defaults via a shared config file.

What happens when my license expires?

You get a 14-day grace period. After that, the app shows a renewal reminder but never locks your data. It's always accessible.

Is this affiliated with Salesforce?

No. Workforce Timetracker is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Salesforce, Inc.

What if I switch machines?

Copy the .exe and your data file to the new machine. Done. Your license key works on any number of your own devices.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes — €48/year (save €12). Contact us after downloading for an annual license key.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet — currently Windows 10/11 only. A Mac version is on the roadmap based on demand.
