    What these numbers can and cannot tell you

    • Observations are scheduled every six hours. Recording starts with the first saved baseline; earlier daily or monthly totals cannot be reconstructed.
    • Date-range totals use only complete intervals between saved observations inside your selected dates. Requests at uncovered start/end boundaries are not estimated.
    • All recorded release asset IDs are retained in history. New versions contribute alongside older versions; missing assets, counter decreases and missing baselines are flagged rather than silently treated as zero traffic.
    • Automated requests, repeat downloads and your own downloads may be included. Direct links to legacy ZIP/DMG files hosted on this website bypass GitHub counters.
    • Cloudflare Web Analytics separately reports website and download-page visits. Choose this site and Exclude bots = Yes. Reports may be sampled and missed by privacy tools. Do not divide these different metrics to claim a verified conversion rate.
    • No visitor identifier, cookie or desktop-app telemetry is added by this counter.
